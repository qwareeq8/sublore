# Privacy policy

Sublore has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, or ads. Its developer receives
no data from it.

## What leaves your browser

When you click **Check** beside a Reddit commenter, Sublore sends that
commenter's username and a date range to the
[Arctic Shift](https://github.com/ArthurHeitmann/arctic_shift) API at
`arctic-shift.photon-reddit.com`. On a subreddit thread, it may send one more
request for the same username with a 7-day range. Requests carry no cookies,
page URL, page content, or watchlist. Arctic Shift receives your IP address, as
with any web request.

Nothing is sent until you allow lookups in settings, and then only when you
click **Check**, **Retry**, or the recheck button beside a commenter.

## What stays in your browser

Sublore stores your settings and up to 500 cached lookups (each checked
username with its subreddit names and counts) in the browser's extension
storage. **Clear cache** in settings deletes the cache. Removing the extension
deletes everything.

## Limited use

Sublore uses this data only to show subreddit activity beside commenters. It
does not sell the data, transfer it anywhere except Arctic Shift for the
lookup, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness. This
use adheres to the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/),
including the Limited Use requirements.
